Care Plan

Multidisciplinary execution plan — how approved treatment is coordinated across departments. Distinct from the Treatment Plan (the signed clinical strategy).

Treatment Plan

The signed anti-cancer treatment strategy. Only an authorized oncologist of the matching modality may sign, amend, or discontinue it.

Procedures & Notes

Bedside/outpatient procedures performed outside the operating theatre (e.g. bone marrow biopsy, lumbar puncture) — distinct from the surgical operative note.

Treatment Completion

End-of-treatment clinical review, modality reconciliation, and the Cancer Treatment Summary — closes out a course of care and hands off to what comes next.

Oral Therapy

Prescribe self-administered anticancer therapy with the same rigour as an infusion order — counselling, dispensing, and adherence review are handled by Oncology Pharmacy.

Surveillance / Survivorship

Post-treatment follow-up — plans, visits, investigations, late effects, recurrence re-entry, recall, and referrals.

Inpatient Oncology

Admission, oncology H&P, problem list, systemic-therapy-linked MAR, ward rounds, nursing flowsheet, deterioration/escalation, goals of care, transfer/handover, discharge summary, and death documentation.

Toxicity Register

Every toxicity term grouped with its peak/latest grade and timeline (SCR-TOX-001), plus Serious/Reportable Adverse Event filing (SCR-TOX-004).

Response Tracking

Structured, longitudinal per-lesion measurements (baseline/nadir/current) and distinct Progression/Recurrence events.
